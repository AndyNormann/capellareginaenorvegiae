# Surface: Home (both languages)

**Scope:** home page `src/pages/[lang]/index.astro` · **Visitor mode:** Persuade

**Audience & job:** visitors arriving from Instagram, Google search, and Google Maps. They must learn what the chapel is and how to visit, in seconds, from a page led by real photography with minimal text.

**Action / proof:** primary action is "How to get here" → Google Maps (handles directions). Also confirmed: the chapel is always open; questions go to Instagram. Proof is real finished-state photography (a better batch is pending from the client); no logistics are invented.

**Direction:** "The Open Door" — photo-led sequence: full-bleed interior hero with a simple welcome and the map CTA, a photo gallery with one what-is line, a getting-here panel (map, always open, Instagram), and a full-bleed best-light close with Instagram handle and a small support link. Story, about, and support recede to quiet links/nav.

**Memorable moment:** the first viewport — light through stone, a simple welcome, one action.

**Constraints:** incumbent design system fixed (tokens, Newsreader/Epilogue/Plus Jakarta/Caveat, 8-theme switcher, Material icons); no/en parity; donations are secondary (full fundraiser lives on the support page); copy is under revision — wording is provisional, facts confirmed.

**Open decisions:** the pinned Google Maps URL for Ledum farm (currently a search-query placeholder, marked TODO); final welcome/wording; the incoming photo batch drops into the fixed composition.
